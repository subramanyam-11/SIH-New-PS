## Second-by-Second Presenter Script

### Step 1 (0:00 – 0:25): Submission & Automated Path Routing
- **Presenter Action:** Open `/login`. Click the fast persona chip **"Principal (Apex Kanpur)"** (`principal@apexkanpur.demo.example`). Click Login.
- **Screen:** `/portal` (Institution Portal)
- **Presenter Speaks:**  
  > *"Respected Jury, welcome. Every year, over ten thousand technical institutions apply for AICTE approval. We begin from the institution's perspective. Here, Apex Institute of Technology in Kanpur has submitted application APP-10001 for a new engineering college.  
  > Immediately upon submission, our rule engine deterministically assigned the statutory pathway—Scrutiny, Re-Scrutiny, EVC, and SAC review—and calculated the SLA calendar based on working days, automatically excluding gazetted holidays."*

---

### Step 2 (0:25 – 0:50): Scrutiny Clearance & Conflict-Free EVC Panel Selection
- **Presenter Action:** Top-right profile menu -> Switch to **"Scrutiny Officer 1 (North)"** (`scrutiny1@demo.example`). Navigate to `/evc-queue`.
- **Screen:** `/evc-queue`
- **Presenter Speaks:**  
  > *"We now switch to the Northern Regional Scrutiny Officer. Case APP-10021 has cleared document scrutiny and entered the Expert Visit Committee queue.  
  > Notice the automated panel recommender. It screens 15 verified academic experts across disciplines, cross-checks their parent state and institutional affiliation, and flags conflicts of interest. The officer selects a verified, conflict-free three-member panel and schedules the inspection for next Tuesday in one click."*

---

### Step 3 (0:50 – 1:15): Early Delay Risk Warning & Explainable Breakdown
- **Presenter Action:** Switch to **"Regional Head (North)"** (`ro.north@demo.example`). Navigate to `/` (Regional Executive Dashboard).
- **Screen:** `/` (Dashboard -> High Risk Applications Card)
- **Presenter Speaks:**  
  > *"Next, the Regional Head logs in. Rather than waiting for deadlines to expire, our system computes an explainable Delay Risk Score from zero to one hundred for every active case.  
  > For application APP-10033, the system flags a High Risk score of 70.7. Clicking the badge reveals the exact four-factor breakdown: 35 points for calendar age, 20 points for multiple document returns, 10 points for officer queue congestion, and 5.7 points for baseline EVC stage duration. This provides actionable early warning four weeks before statutory pendency occurs."*

### Step 5 (1:35 – 1:55): Deficiency Return & Fair Pendency Clock

- **Screen:** `/applications/1` (Timeline and Clock Card)
- **Presenter Speaks:**  
  > *"A critical issue in technical education governance is accountability: did a case stall at AICTE, or was the college slow to respond?  
  > When the officer returns APP-10001 for a deficiency, the case holder immediately switches to 'WITH INSTITUTION'. The statutory AICTE pendency clock pauses. The institution cannot be penalized for internal AICTE delays, and AICTE officers cannot be held liable for delayed college replies. Once the institution resubmits, the clock resumes."*
